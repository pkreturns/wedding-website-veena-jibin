# Burgundy reference artwork

The silk envelope and candle banner were extracted from the user-supplied reference video. The invitation typography and all event details are live HTML.

Two project assets were made using the built-in image-generation tool:

- `assets/lace-invitation.png`: reference-guided blank ivory crocheted-lace invitation on an opened burgundy embossed floral envelope, on handmade ivory paper; no text or record.
- `assets/gold-frame.png`: reference-guided complete front-facing antique gold Rococo portrait frame with blank burgundy paper inside and transparent exterior; no words or props.

The floral spray and music are reused from the original project. The couple’s actual pictures have not yet been supplied; add them to `photos.js` to activate the photo gallery. No sample couple photographs are presented as Praveena and Jibin.

The layout follows the reference’s envelope, invitation collage, story page, framed venue, map, ceremony panel, and FAQ sections. RSVP is omitted. Unconfirmed sample timings, hotel bookings, and gift registry links from the video are not represented as real event details.
